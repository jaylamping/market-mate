# Supervisory Overview

- **Mode:** Operate.
- **Audience and job:** The Principal must understand safety, attention, after-cost performance, capital, costs, and evidence within ten seconds, then inspect any claim without losing global context.
- **Proof and constraints:** Real preserved evidence only; Research, Paper, and Live remain distinct; the dashboard is owner-only, display-only, read-only, WCAG 2.2 AA, and never an order path.
- **Chosen direction:** Dark institutional risk dashboard with Linear as the craft bar and shadcn-style composable hierarchy. Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/dashboard-matrix-evidence.png`.
- **Memorable moment:** Four immediate answers sit above performance and attention, while a persistent evidence dock keeps custody and lineage in view.
- **Open decisions:** Deeper-route compositions remain intentionally deferred until the dashboard shell proves its hierarchy.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** One heads-up matrix answers the Principal’s four critical questions before detail; it refuses an undifferentiated card wall.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm near-black ground, charcoal elevations, softened separators, off-white type, restrained blue, and semantic amber/red/green with non-color cues.

**STORY:** Confirm trust, identify attention, read after-cost performance and capital, then inspect preserved evidence without losing context.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Compact sidebar and authority strip; four answer sections; performance chart beside ranked attention; a full-width two-cycle evidence dock with lineage links.

**FORM:** Evidence Dock, refinement C of the selected Heads-Up Matrix; seed `56811b56`.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
