Acceptance run for every candidate

  1. Disable public signup and bind one immutable provider subject to the internal Principal ID; reject email-only matching and account relinking.
  2. Enroll a Face ID-backed Safari passkey plus a separate roaming hardware key; prove loss-of-phone recovery without email-only takeover.
  3. Revoke the active session and authenticator; measure propagation through API checks and signed events, including provider outage behavior.
  4. Issue a Market Mate challenge over the exact proposal digest, binding version, authority epoch, audience, expiry, and nonce; prove replay and proposal mutation fail.
  5. Exercise web and native-compatible step-up, deep-link return, device replacement, event export/replay, tenant export, rate limits, support path, and fully loaded monthly cost.
