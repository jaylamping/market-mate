# Alpaca as Market Mate's primary Paper and first Live platform

Research date: 2026-08-24
Scope: Alpaca's individual Trading API for a Kansas-resident Principal, approximately $1,000 of trading capital, U.S. equities, and defined-risk options. Broker API partner features are not assumed to exist for an individual account.
Method: current first-party Alpaca documentation, API references, support, status, and disclosure pages only. Product documentation establishes **Documented** behavior; only retained executable tests can establish **Observed** behavior.

## Recommendation

**Make Alpaca Market Mate's preferred first qualification target: the intended primary Paper Execution Venue after certification and the leading candidate for first Restricted Live.** “Preferred” changes certification order, not the outcome. The Alpaca adapter, qualification suite, and operating runbooks should run first, but a better-qualified Live venue may win; Alpaca is neither pre-certified nor an exclusive broker.

This is stronger than the earlier “Alpaca and Tradier are equal front-runners” posture. Alpaca now combines the same Paper/Live API schema, separate environment credentials and hosts, unattended API-key operation, live atomic multi-leg equity/ETF options, a documented options margin model, and order WebSockets. Alpaca also added SPX/SPXW/VIX/VIXW/DJX/XSP to **Paper** in July 2026. However, Alpaca's current Live product page still says only U.S. equity and ETF options are available and index options are “coming soon”; Paper index-option results therefore cannot establish a Live capability. [Paper trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading), [Trading API authentication](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/authentication), [Level 3 multi-leg options](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-level-3-trading), [index options in Paper](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-introduces-index-options-paper-trading/), [current options product](https://alpaca.markets/options)

The correct decision is therefore **Alpaca-first, evidence-gated, and broker-neutral**. If Alpaca fails a hard gate for a required capability, Market Mate falls back to another qualified adapter rather than weakening the gate.

## Decision-grade findings

| Area | Current evidence | Consequence for Market Mate |
|---|---|---|
| **Paper/live contract parity** | Alpaca says Paper uses the same API specification as Live, but separate keys and hosts. Paper is the same end-to-end system except that orders are simulated instead of routed to an exchange. Product eligibility still differs: index options are currently Paper-only. [Paper trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading), [index options in Paper](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-introduces-index-options-paper-trading/), [current options product](https://alpaca.markets/options) | High adapter-contract parity, not economic or capability parity. Preserve separate Paper/Live venue identities, credentials, ledgers, certification leases, and capability manifests even when both say Alpaca. |
| **Equities and order operation** | Trading API supports U.S. stocks, fractional shares, market/limit/stop and more complex order classes; fractional eligibility remains asset-specific. Responses carry an `X-Request-ID` that Alpaca recommends persisting for support. [Trading API](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/trading-api), [request IDs](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/getting-started-with-trading-api) | Suitable first target for stock orchestration and minimum-size Live calibration. Persist venue request, order, execution, and client IDs in the Decision Record. |
| **Options and multi-leg semantics** | Paper options are enabled by default; Live requires an options approval level. Level 3 supports live multi-leg orders and documents a four-leg iron condor as filling together or not at all. MLegs cannot contain equity legs, ratios must be reduced, and uncovered legs inside the MLeg are rejected. Alpaca's current public Live product is equity/ETF options, American style; Paper additionally includes named cash-settled index options. [Options trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-trading), [Level 3](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-level-3-trading), [index options in Paper](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-introduces-index-options-paper-trading/), [current options product](https://alpaca.markets/options) | Certify stock, long option, vertical, and four-leg equity/ETF packages independently. Do not infer Live SPX/XSP, box, equity-leg combo, uncovered-leg, or lifecycle capability from Paper acceptance. |
| **Paper fill fidelity** | The simulator ignores market impact, information leakage, latency slippage, queue position, price improvement, regulatory fees, dividends, borrow fees, and displayed NBBO size. Eligible orders receive a random-size partial fill 10% of the time. [Paper specification](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading) | Alpaca Paper is excellent for orchestration but raw Paper P&L is not promotion-grade economics. The Conservative Simulation Overlay remains mandatory; Alpaca's explicit omissions supply its initial adverse parameters. |
| **Market data** | Basic is free but gives IEX-only equities and an indicative options feed; Algo Trader Plus is $99/month and provides consolidated U.S. equities and OPRA. The indicative options quotes are modified derivatives, while trades are delayed 15 minutes. Historical options data begins in February 2024. [Market-data plans](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api), [historical options data](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/historical-option-data) | Basic may validate integration, not execution economics. Qualification and promotion need licensed point-in-time SIP/OPRA evidence. Decide separately whether Alpaca Plus or a provider-neutral feed supplies it; do not let broker choice silently choose the evidence source. |
| **Authentication and cloud automation** | Individual Trading API supports separate static key/secret pairs for Paper and Live; Alpaca's own CLI documents environment-variable use for CI and agents. Client-credentials tokens and Broker API credential controls are not available to Trading API. OAuth can scope third-party access to trading/data/account-write, but requires an authorization flow. Public individual-account docs do not show a fine-grained static key that is cryptographically limited to trading with funding disabled. [Authentication](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/authentication), [OAuth scopes](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/using-oauth2-and-trading-api), [CLI automation](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/alpacas-cli) | Strong unattended-cloud fit, with a least-privilege gap to resolve. Store Paper and Live secrets separately, default deployment to Paper, require environment binding and secret rotation, and treat withdrawal-proof credential scope as unproven until Alpaca confirms it or an OAuth design is validated. |
| **Exercise, assignment, expiration** | Alpaca documents API exercise and DNE, automatic exercise at $0.01 ITM, pre-expiration sell-outs when buying power is insufficient, and overnight assignment risk. Assignment is not emitted on the legacy order WebSocket and must be reconciled through activities; in Paper, balances/positions update immediately but option non-trade activities may not appear until the next day. [Options trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-trading), [options overview](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-trading-overview), [exercise endpoint](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/optionexercise), [DNE endpoint](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/optiondonotexercise) | Lifecycle automation is plausible but not certified. Poll/replay authoritative activities, model sell-out and overnight assignment, and prove DNE cutoffs, assignment locks, expiration, cash settlement, and reconnect behavior per instrument class before granting that capability. |
| **Corporate actions** | Alpaca's account-activity schema enumerates mergers, reorganizations, symbol changes, spinoffs and splits, and Alpaca cancels affected GTC orders for events such as splits, symbol/CUSIP changes, and mergers. Its corporate-actions data endpoint warns that provider and processing delays can occur and gives no creation-time guarantee. Paper does not simulate dividends. [account activities](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/account-activities), [mandatory corporate actions](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/mandatory-corporate-actions), [corporate-actions API warning](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1), [Paper specification](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading) | Good reconciliation vocabulary, not a timing guarantee. Keep Security Master and Corporate Action Case evidence provider-neutral; indeterminate deliverables remain a fail-closed lifecycle condition. |
| **Buying power, margin, and fees** | Individual Live accounts are opened as margin accounts, but below $2,000 they have 1x buying power and cannot margin-trade or short. Options use separate buying power; Level 3 margin is based on combined theoretical maximum loss plus net package price. Retail API options have no commission, but TAF, ORF, OCC, CAT and other applicable charges remain and are accrued intraday then charged at end of day. [Trading account](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/account-plans), [margin](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/margin-and-short-selling), [Level 3 margin](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-level-3-trading), [regulatory fees](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/regulatory-fees), [current fee schedule](https://files.alpaca.markets/disclosures/library/BrokFeeSched.pdf) | The approximately $1,000 account is effectively cash/limited-margin and cannot assume short stock or borrowed buying power. Reconcile accrued and posted fees separately; every Minimum Executable Unit must still fit the existing risk ceiling after premium, maximum loss, fees, and lifecycle funding. |
| **Events, statements, and reconciliation** | `trade_updates` emits fills, partial fills, cancellations, and rejects. Alpaca's newer beta Activity SSE schema covers fills, corrections/busts, fees, transfers, corporate actions, and option lifecycle events, but its integration guide currently instructs use of Broker API credentials while a May 2026 changelog lists a Trading API activity endpoint. Treat individual-account availability as unresolved. The public Documents API is also explicitly Broker API; individual Trading API tax documents are documented in the dashboard. No retail webhook contract was found; the documented paths are streams plus REST. [order WebSocket](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/websocket-streaming), [Activity SSE](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/activity-sse), [Trading API activity changelog](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/changelog/2026-05-05-activity-types-697a81d), [statements and confirms](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/statements-and-confirms), [Trading-account tax documents](https://alpaca.markets/support/tax-documents) | Use WebSocket + REST activities + account/position/order snapshots, not one channel as the ledger. Dual-run the beta Activity SSE only if the actual Trading account accepts it. Before Live, prove machine-reconcilable confirmations/statements or retain a controlled manual import fallback. |
| **Availability and limits** | Alpaca publishes a component/incident/maintenance status API. Market-data WebSocket connections are subscription-limited and most plans allow one connection per endpoint. The public product page advertises 99.99% uptime since January 2025, but this is not a contractual SLA or proof for Market Mate's exact workflows. [status API](https://status.alpaca.markets/api), [WebSocket connection limit](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/streaming-market-data), [options product](https://alpaca.markets/options) | Treat disconnects, duplicate/missing events, status degradation, and unknown order outcomes as normal failure modes. Certification needs measured recovery, REST reconciliation, backoff, outage fencing, and broker-native intervention handling. |
| **Account eligibility** | A U.S. applicant must be at least 18, have an SSN, legal U.S. residential address, and qualifying citizenship/residency/visa status. Live approval and Level 3 options approval are individualized; Alpaca can approve a lower level or reject, limits initial applications, and requires a wait before reapplication. Individual accounts are taxable, not retirement accounts. [U.S. eligibility](https://alpaca.markets/support/requirements-alpaca-brokerage-account), [options approval factors](https://alpaca.markets/support/what-factors-does-alpaca-consider-when-reviewing-and-approving-options-applications-and-trading-levels), [account plans](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/account-plans) | Kansas residency is not a documented exclusion, but neither Live approval nor Level 3 may be presumed. Capture actual account type, restrictions, options level, eligible assets, and funding state before qualification. |

## Effect on existing Market Mate decisions

### Change now

1. **Promote Alpaca from co-front-runner to preferred first qualification target.** Build and certify the broker-neutral Alpaca adapter first for stock Paper, options Paper, and smallest-size stock Live. If Level 3 approval and executable-unit affordability succeed, add defined-risk equity/ETF options Live one capability at a time. A competing Live venue may still win on the same hard gates.
2. **Make the first Live calibration Alpaca-first, not Alpaca-guaranteed.** The Live gate should nominate `Alpaca / exact account / exact adapter version / exact capability`, with an automatic fallback to another candidate if any hard gate fails.
3. **Add an explicit Paper-only index-options capability.** Alpaca Paper now supports SPX, SPXW, VIX, VIXW, DJX, and XSP, while current first-party Live material says index options are not yet available. Preserve those observations as simulator and adapter evidence only. [index options in Paper](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-introduces-index-options-paper-trading/), [current options product](https://alpaca.markets/options)
4. **Add two Alpaca-specific qualification gaps:** prove least-privilege credentials for an individual account, and prove automated retrieval or controlled import of individual statements/trade confirmations.

### Remain unchanged

- **Broker-neutral adapter:** retained. Alpaca-first is sequencing, not architectural lock-in.
- **Separate Paper and Live venues:** retained even when both use Alpaca, because keys, hosts, custody, fills, lifecycle timing, capabilities, and evidence differ.
- **Conservative Simulation Overlay:** strengthened, not relaxed. Alpaca's paper-fill omissions make it indispensable.
- **Capability-scoped certification:** retained. Stock, fractional stock, single-leg options, equity/ETF MLeg, index-options Paper, exercise/DNE, assignment, expiration, and corporate actions qualify independently.
- **Minimum-size later Live calibration:** retained. Documentation itself recommends small Live trades to expose simulator omissions, but only after the existing promotion, approval, affordability, risk, and operator gates pass. [Paper trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading)
- **No box spreads initially:** retained. Alpaca Paper index support and its universal-spread margin method do not prove Live SPX/XSP availability, account approval, routing, fills, settlement, or positive after-cost economics. Equity-option boxes would also introduce American-style early-assignment risk.

## Alpaca-first qualification stop rules

Alpaca becomes the first Live venue only after retained tests prove: environment binding cannot cross Paper/Live; every order and cash event reconciles after disconnect; static credentials meet an accepted least-privilege threat model; paid or external SIP/OPRA evidence is licensed and timestamped; actual Level 3/account permissions are captured; the intended Minimum Executable Unit fits both broker buying power and Market Mate risk limits; lifecycle events and operator interventions reconcile; and smallest-size Live fills establish tolerances for slippage, fees, rejects, partial fills, and timing.

Failure of any hard gate changes the selected venue or certified capability. It does not change the gate.
